# Block Blast (Hungry Studio) — Research vs Our Build

Research date: 2026-11-03. Primary sources first (Play listing, App Store listing, blockblast.com); high-trust write-ups only where primaries are silent. Every factual claim cites its source inline.

## 1. Core rules

- **8×8 board.** Google Play: "Place colorful blocks on the 8x8 board, clear rows and columns" (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle&hl=en`). App Store: "Match colorful blocks on an 8x8 board" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`).
- **3-piece batches, drag-and-drop, no gravity/clock.** WikiHow (sourced from gameplay): "starts with an empty 8 x 8 grid… Each round, you're given 3 random block shapes… Click and drag the blocks onto empty spaces where they fit" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Forbes describes the same pick-up loop ("picked it up… hours had passed as I tried to beat my high score", `https://www.forbes.com/sites/mikestubbs/2024/10/20/i-lost-an-entire-saturday-to-block-blast-the-hit-mobile-puzzle-game/`).
- **Clear condition: full rows OR columns only.** Google Play "How to Play: Drag blocks onto the board to create full rows or columns" (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle&hl=en`). App Store: "Complete rows or columns of tiles and watch the block crush effects cascade" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`). No same-color matching, no 3×3-square bonus documented in either primary listing.
- **Combo/streak scoring.** Google Play: "Clear blocks to score points and trigger exciting combos" (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle&hl=en`). App Store: "Chase Combos: Plan your moves to unlock massive points" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`). Mechanics per write-ups: WikiHow — completing a line "adds to your combo multiplier"; "if you finish a line with the 3 blocks you're given during the round, the multiplier increases by 1… If you don't complete a line, then the combo multiplier drops back down to 0" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Hofstra Chronicle (student paper, detailed mechanics): "A combo begins when a player continually blasts blocks within three moves of the last blast… three moves to be able to complete a combo… If they fail… the combo 'count' resets"; author reached 12 combos (`https://thehofstrachronicle.com/103974/features/blast-away-your-sleep-schedule-with-block-blast/`). Hofstra scoring numbers: "five points for placing a block… 10 to three hundred points for blasting away rows… dependent on whether the player completes a 'combo'" (same URL) — treat exact numbers as write-up claims, not primary.
- **Placement points.** WikiHow: "you earn points based on how many squares it contains. If you complete a full row or column… you earn at least 10 points" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`).
- **Game over: no remaining placement fits.** WikiHow: "If you ever run out of empty space on the grid and can't place any of your blocks, the game immediately ends" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Classic mode is endless until that point ("Classic Mode has endless levels, so you just play until you lose", same URL).
- **Assist details (real game, undocumented in listings):** game highlights fitting areas if you wait ("the game also highlights potential areas where one of your blocks fits", `https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`); repeated losses trigger pity help — "losing multiple times activates a 'God Mode' that drops ideal pieces" (`https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`).

## 2. Piece set

- **Shapes: Tetris-like family** — "three block shapes at a time — anything from a single square to an L-piece or a straight line" (`https://walkthroughs.net/block-blast-modes-combos-and-tips-explained`). WikiHow strategy advises "leave space for larger blocks, such as 3 x 3 squares and L-pieces" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`).
- **No rotation.** Neither store listing nor WikiHow's how-to mentions rotation; all placement is drag-only. No rotate button/booster appears in primary sources. (Rotate boosters in search results belong to other block games / clones, not Hungry Studio's listings.)
- **Dealt as random 3-piece trays; pieces persist until cleared by lines.** WikiHow: "Any blocks you place stay on the grid until you fill all the squares in the same line or column" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`).

## 3. Game modes

- **Classic (endless high-score).** App Store: "Endless High Score Hunt: Push yourself further with each game — break your own records" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`). WikiHow: "Classic Mode has endless levels, so you just play until you lose" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`).
- **Adventure (progressive levels).** App Store: "Adventure Mode: Experience progressive levels with unique jigsaw designs and colorful themes" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`). WikiHow: "Adventure Mode presents you with different levels… The grids may have different shapes or the blocks may have special powers… refreshes frequently with new, unique challenges" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Hofstra: adventure adds collect-goals — "diamonds and gold… 'embedded' within the blocks. Players collect them when they blast those specific blocks. Failure to collect the correct number… results in a game loss"; completing all levels "within the allotted time, which is usually about seven days, they receive a trophy" (`https://thehofstrachronicle.com/103974/features/blast-away-your-sleep-schedule-with-block-blast/`). Balancy (Mar 2025): "Adventure mode, available for seven days… completing 96 levels to reveal a pixelated image, one pixel at a time. However, there is no promised reward and no collection system" (`https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`).
- **Daily puzzles.** Google Play lists "Daily Brain Training Challenges… new puzzles every day" (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle&hl=en`). App Store: "Daily Puzzle Variety: Every day brings a new puzzle twist… new rewards to earn" + "Daily Puzzle Rewards" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`).
- **No timed mode documented** in primary listings (no timer; "Take your time placing blocks… close out of the app and come back later", `https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). No tournament bracket in primary sources; competition is framed as friends/leaderboard (see §4).

## 4. Meta systems

- **Coins/currency: none documented.** Balancy (Mar 2025): "No game shop… No resources like energy or lives. Players can play as long as they want" (`https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`). No currency appears on either store listing.
- **Boosters (hammer/rotate/shuffle): NOT in primary sources.** Neither listing names any booster. WikiHow mentions only adventure "special powers" on blocks, no player-inventory boosters (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Booster guides in search results (windmill/hammer/bomb/undo) describe other titles or clones — do not treat as Block Blast features.
- **Revive/continue: yes, via rewarded ad.** WikiHow Q&A: "To use the revive feature, ensure your Wi-Fi is on. Once you click the revive button, you will need to watch an ad, and then you can continue playing" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Balancy: "Rewarded video ads only for reviving after losing… revival is allowed only once per game session" (Mar 2025; `https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`). Reddit r/blockblast (Feb 2025): "In the newer updates, you can revive yourself up to 3 times. In the older update… only once" (`https://www.reddit.com/r/blockblast/comments/1ijrcjy/why_does_everyone_have_a_different_type_of_block/`) — version-dependent, A/B-tested.
- **Daily rewards:** App Store promises "Daily Puzzle Rewards… new rewards to earn" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`); exact reward content unspecified.
- **Seasons/events:** time-limited Adventure windows (7 days / ~96 levels, Balancy URL above); official offline event "GameVerse Vietnam 2026… Block City, live PK challenges" (`https://www.blockblast.com/`); 2026 Webby People's Voice win (same URL). No persistent season-pass in primary sources; Udonis notes "no battle pass, no aggressive store" (`https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast`).
- **Leaderboard/friends:** App Store "Score Mastery: Compete against friends, share your results" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`). Balancy: post-win "social proof… displays a percentage showing how many players have been surpassed" after hard levels (`https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`). No global-tournament detail in primaries.
- **Profile/avatars:** official site shows purple/red block-character avatar art (`https://www.blockblast.com/`), but no profile/avatar-customization system is documented in listings. (App Store reviews mention a color-palette/image-import feature appearing in ads but not found in-game — `https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485?platform=iphone&see-all=reviews` — treat as ads-vs-game discrepancy, not a confirmed feature.)
- **Win streaks:** tracked as a counter only — "calculates win-streaks, but only to show the counter… no rewards for milestones or penalties" (`https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`).
- **Cloud/account:** nothing documented in primaries; best-score chase is device-local in listings' framing ("break your own records", App Store URL above).

## 5. Juice / feel

- **Block-crush cascade + music:** App Store: "watch vibrant cubes explode in color", "Stunning Block Effects: Feel the rush of every block crush with dynamic visuals and relaxing music effects" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`). Google Play: "satisfying visual effects", "Smooth & Relaxing Gameplay… vibrant visuals and satisfying effects" (`https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.block.juggle&hl=en`).
- **Confetti + encouragement text:** Hofstra: "From the colors of the blocks, to the confetti and the words of encouragement, the game is very visually stimulating. The colors include light and dark blue, red, yellow, orange, green and pink" (`https://thehofstrachronicle.com/103974/features/blast-away-your-sleep-schedule-with-block-blast/`).
- **Combo callouts:** New Trier News: the combo elements ("the 'Combo 4' and the heart"), and the sparkly graphics make the game addicting (`https://newtriernews.org/entertainment/2024/11/06/whats-the-deal-with-block-blast/`).
- **Themes/skins, haptics, dark mode:** none documented in primary sources. No theme/skin menu, haptics toggle, or dark-mode claim appears on either listing or blockblast.com.

## 6. Monetization surfaces (we EXCLUDE all of these — reference only)

- **Ad-only model (2023–2025):** "relying entirely on ad monetization: static banners at the bottom; interstitial ads after every level — short and skippable; rewarded video ads only for reviving" (`https://balancy.co/blog/2025/03/26/how-could-block-blast-by-hungry-studio-earn-more-monetization-and-gameplay-deconstruction/`). Scale: "~$17.5 million per month… ~$584,000 per day, from ads" with "lifetime IAP revenue… around $66,000" (`https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast`); "~$127M in ad revenue, Jan–May 2026, the most of any mobile game" (`https://famekite.com/resources/block-blast-growth-teardown/`).
- **No-ads option absent (2025), subscriptions by 2026:** Balancy: "No 'No Ads' purchase option" (Mar 2025, URL above); WikiHow: "doesn't offer the option to turn off ads or purchase an ad-free version… airplane mode to prevent ads" (`https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`). Current App Store listing now shows IAP subscriptions (1 Week $4.99 / 1 Month $9.99 / 1 Year $49.99) and "Contains Advertising" (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`) — subscriptions postdate the write-ups.
- **Apple Arcade variant:** "Block Blast!+ … joined Apple Arcade on September 3, 2026… with no ads or in-app purchases" (`https://www.blockblast.com/`).
- **No energy/lives/shop:** "No game shop and special offers. No resources like energy or lives" (Balancy URL above).
- **Airplane-mode caveat:** offline play is advertised ("No WiFi Needed", Google Play URL), but revive requires connection (WikiHow Q&A: "If you turn off your Wi-Fi, the revive feature may disappear", `https://www.wikihow.com/Play-Block-Blast`).

## 7. Addictive core (review consensus)

- **"One more run" high-score chase:** Forbes: "I just lost almost an entire Saturday to it… hours had passed as I tried to beat my high score" (`https://www.forbes.com/sites/mikestubbs/2024/10/20/i-lost-an-entire-saturday-to-block-blast-the-hit-mobile-puzzle-game/`). Gaming.net: "a simple game that serves as a guilty pleasure… anyone can jump in… without needing any prior experience" (`https://www.gaming.net/reviews/block-blast-review-android-ios/`).
- **Combo + sparkle payoff:** "Combo 4" callouts + heart + "sparkly graphics make the game addicting" (`https://newtriernews.org/entertainment/2024/11/06/whats-the-deal-with-block-blast/`); Hofstra author chased "12 combos in a row, which is no easy feat" (`https://thehofstrachronicle.com/103974/features/blast-away-your-sleep-schedule-with-block-blast/`).
- **Frictionless loop:** no timer, unlimited sessions, offline-capable (Play listing + Balancy URLs above); TikTok virality/word-of-mouth spread ("mainly spreads through word-of-mouth or seeing others playing it", New Trier URL above; TikTok trend framing, Hofstra URL above).
- **Scale signal:** most-downloaded mobile game worldwide in 2024 and 2025, ~870M lifetime downloads (`https://www.blog.udonis.co/statistics/block-blast`); 4.9★ / ~2.8M ratings on App Store (`https://apps.apple.com/us/app/block-blast/id1617391485`).

## GAP ANALYSIS — Block Blast vs our build (`index.html`)

Our build: single-file 8×8 clone — rows+cols clear, combo streak, weighted 3-piece batches, 2 themes (cute/classic), local best score, WebAudio tones, `navigator.vibrate`, pause/restart, drag + tap-to-place, ghost preview. No ads/IAP by design.

### Verdict
Parity on the core loop; closest gaps worth closing in v1: (1) true combo-reset semantics, (2) idle fit-hint, (3) full-clear/encouragement juice. Everything meta (adventure, dailies, leaderboards, revive, boosters, coins) is either absent from the original or exists to serve ads — correctly out of scope for the no-ads build.
